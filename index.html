Business grounding, new technical toolkit

I'm Roja Rani Repalle, a technology professional with over twelve years of industry experience, much of it spent deep in the e-commerce domain — digital transformation initiatives, customer-facing platforms, and the systems that keep an online business running.

In 2026, I started a focused transition into Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Generative AI, and Agentic AI. Not a reinvention from scratch, but an extension of what I already know — I understand the business problems; now I'm learning to bring modern AI to bear on them.

My goal is to combine that business and e-commerce grounding with practical AI skills — computer vision, OCR, language models, and agentic tooling — to build things that are genuinely useful, not just technically interesting.
